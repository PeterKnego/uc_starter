# What next?

This is the tutor path: 13 steps, in two parts. `make next` (or `scripts/next.sh`)
tells you which step you're on by inspecting the repo — never by memory — so
it's safe to run at any time, from any terminal, or from an agent.

Part 1 gets your app running on a local three-node cluster. Part 2 covers the
operational concerns: snapshots, observability, upgrades and deployment.
